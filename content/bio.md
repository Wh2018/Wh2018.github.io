I am an Assistant Professor at the School of Journalism and New Media, Xi'an Jiaotong University, and a recipient of the university's **Young Outstanding Talent** program. My work spans **LLM agents, agent-based social simulation, intelligent communication, and public opinion analysis and governance**.

I lead projects funded by the **National Natural Science Foundation of China (Youth Science Fund, Category C)** and the **78th Batch General Financial Grant of the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation**. I have published 10+ papers in **CCF A/B** conferences and journals, and **JCR Q1** journals.

Before joining XJTU, I earned both my BSc and PhD at the **School of Computer Science, Northwestern Polytechnical University**, where I was advised by [Prof. Bin Guo](http://guob.org/).

I am always glad to hear from collaborators, students, and anyone curious about this line of work. Please feel free to write to me.
